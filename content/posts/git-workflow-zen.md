## Starting My Journey in Data & AI

Hi, I'm Alyssa! I'm currently studying Data Analytics & AI, and I'm excited to be in a field that blends logic, creativity and real-world impact. What I love most about this program is how versatile it is. Data is everywhere, and the ability to analyze it opens doors in almost every industry imaginable.


### Where My Passion Meets My Purpose

For me, the most exciting application of data analytics is sports. Football specifically. I've always been passionate about the game, and the idea that I can use skills like data visualization, predictive modeling and AI to uncover patterns in player performance or game strategy feels like the perfect intersection of who I am and what I'm learning. It's what made me realize this field was the right fit.


### Building Skills One Dataset at a Time

I'm still early in my journey, but every new concept feels like unlocking a new way to see the world. Working with datasets, identifying trends and learning tools like Python and Excel has already shifted how I think about information. Data isn't just numbers. It tells stories, reveals insights and helps people make smarter decisions.


## What This Blog Is For

This blog will document my growth throughout the program. What I'm learning, challenges I face and the goals I'm working toward. I want to build a career where analytical thinking and creativity come together to solve real problems, and this is where I'll track that journey.
